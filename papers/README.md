# Papers

Papers in progress, public before they are polished — typically spun off from a [research agenda](../agendas/). Substantial contributors are invited as coauthors; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

Papers are numbered MAIS-P1, MAIS-P2, … in order of arrival; numbers are permanent.

| # | Paper | Source | PDF | Status |
|---|-------|--------|-----|--------|
| MAIS-P1 | *Math for AI Safety: An Invitation for Mathematicians* | — | [PDF](P1/MAIS-P1.pdf) | under review · revision in progress |

Each paper lives in its own subfolder. MAIS-P1 is published here as a fixed PDF: it is a solo paper nearing completion, so its source is not a collaboration surface. Later papers, which are, will carry their LaTeX source alongside the PDF; to rebuild one of those, run `latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error FILE.tex` from its folder (LaTeX auxiliary files are ignored).
