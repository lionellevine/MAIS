# July 2026 agenda audit

These records document the first audit and repair round for [MAIS-A1–A8](../../). Each agenda was drafted by Claude Fable 5 under Lionel Levine's direction, reviewed adversarially by a separate AI agent, and audited by GPT 5.6 Sol. The eight Sol reports checked 55 proved or background results and 97 numbered research items. They also conducted and recorded literature searches testing the open-status claims.

All eight Sol reports concern pre-repair drafts and reached the overall verdict `significant-issues`. That is why they are useful: the reports record false statements, missing hypotheses, resolved “open” problems, imprecise formulations, and attribution problems before the repair round. Their item and line numbers are historical.

A separate scoped pass rechecked the 19 findings most capable of invalidating or substantially changing mathematical content. It confirmed 16, narrowed three, and rejected none. Sol then repaired the agendas; a final gate checked fidelity to the accepted dispositions, house style, references, and compilation. The released editions all passed that gate.
