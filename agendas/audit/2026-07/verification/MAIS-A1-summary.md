# Scoped verification summary for MAIS-A1

The verification pass rechecked four destructive or potentially destructive findings in the pre-repair bounded-Löb agenda:

- the symbol cost of repeated-squaring numerals under the agenda's finite-alphabet abbreviation convention;
- the counterexample to the unrestricted bounded-reflection proposition;
- the counterexample to the bounded-outer-box aside;
- the negative resolution of the unrestricted effective-threshold problem.

All four were **confirmed** against the hypotheses and conventions stated in that draft.

A fuller working report is not released because it contains machine-local paths and unpublished coordination material. See the [pre-repair Sol report](../sol/MAIS-A1.md), [released-edition repair log](../REPAIR-LOG.md#mais-a1-quantitative-bounded-löb), and [gate summary](../GATE-SUMMARY.md).

The same pass also verified, line by line, Sol's standalone refutation-and-repair note on the source theorem: the PBL refutation was confirmed; one repairable gap in its cooperation refutation was found (compressible padding under abbreviation systems); and two standing assumptions of its repaired theorem were found to be implicit. The note is released as its author wrote it as [MAIS-P2](../../../../papers/P2/); the repaired statements are incorporated in the released edition of MAIS-A1.
