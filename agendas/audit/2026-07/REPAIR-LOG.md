# Released-edition repair log

This is the publication log for MAIS-A1–A8. It summarizes changes made in response to the July 2026 audits; the linked pre-repair reports contain the evidence and full findings. The dispositions used below are:

- **fixed:** wording, hypotheses, definitions, proof, or attribution was corrected;
- **promoted:** an item posed as open was replaced by a proved statement;
- **counterexample recorded:** a false or overbroad statement was removed or narrowed, with the obstruction credited in an audit note;
- **left open:** the surviving mathematical question was restated after a settled or ill-posed part was removed.

## MAIS-A1: Quantitative bounded Löb

- Specified the proof-file grammar, symbol-length convention, numeral encoding, and canonical budget presentations on which the quantitative statements depend.
- Replaced the unrestricted parametric bounded Löb statement by a conditional theorem with the internal comparison certificates used by its proof. The agenda records the obstruction to mere external growth.
- Reposed the effective-threshold, Löb-window, bounded-reflection, and internal bounded-Löb questions in certified classes; corrected the abbreviation cost and the bounded-outer-box claim.
- Corrected the Payor attribution and narrowed the mechanization, robustness, and state-of-the-art claims.

## MAIS-A2: Behavioral tomography of world-models

- Restored observation masking to the shifted-task family in the Richens–Everitt specialization and recorded the two-variable counterexample to the fixed-observation version.
- Propagated that correction through the behavioral transform, identification, regret, noisy-query, Boltzmann, and restricted-intervention formulations.
- Made the composite-goal family finite and repaired the myopic converse, finite-sample hypotheses, zero-regret endpoint, and persistent-corruption quantifiers.
- Added the directly related 2026 inverse-Bellman work without presenting it as a solution to the policy-bit problems.

## MAIS-A3: The geometry and identifiability of superposition

- Added the overcomplete hypothesis missing from the sparse-recovery conjecture and recorded the slanted-dictionary counterexample to its former version.
- Promoted the positive-penalty merging conjecture to a proposition with proof.
- Kept the column-sparse uniqueness problem open while separating it from nearby recovery theorems with different hypotheses.
- Corrected the tied-ISTA claim, the identifiability data, the pentagon computations, and the experimental starter protocols.

## MAIS-A4: Training for interpretability

- Promoted the thermodynamic-limit conjecture to a proposition using the Fekete lower bound and a block-padding upper bound.
- Corrected the Welch-bound consequence and narrowed claims about solved toy models and large-system ansätze.
- Defined attainment, frontier, and dynamical outcomes precisely, including nonconvergence and the chosen Clarke trajectory.
- Added the nearest 2025–2026 theory and preserved the surviving capacity-saturation and bottleneck-frontier problems.

## MAIS-A5: Which irreducible representations does training select?

- Separated exact cross-entropy and Euclidean decay from Taylor-surrogate flows and other regularizers.
- Restricted the imported asymptotic selection and maximum-margin results to the regimes in which they were proved.
- Corrected the convergence discussion to include the basin of the zero network and the failure of normalized logits there.
- Made the symmetry, limit, and purity quantifiers explicit and replaced the empirical starter by a reproducible protocol.

## MAIS-A6: Does geometric simplicity force a legible mechanism?

- Added the analytic-domain, prior, nontriviality, and local/global conventions needed by the learning-coefficient statements.
- Replaced the uniform-attention problem by a worked exact reduction to reduced-rank regression, including the resulting learning-coefficient formula.
- Recorded the fixed-temperature and hard-ball WBIC consequences, leaving the growing-localization and full local-fiber questions open.
- Proved the finite-input ReLU statement by activation-cell decomposition and reposed the question for continuous inputs.

## MAIS-A7: Effective loss and learning dynamics

- Restored the hypotheses of the Watanabe and positive-level statements and corrected the deep-linear parity and rank conditions.
- Promoted lower semicontinuity of the local real log canonical threshold from a question to a cited theorem.
- Repaired the multiplicative-noise initial condition, specified the stopped diffusion at singular strata, and defined the requested nonautonomous limits.
- Replaced fixed-crossover asymptotics by moving-spectrum and joint-limit formulations with explicit scales.

## MAIS-A8: A predictive theory of out-of-distribution generalization

- Replaced the nonunique finite-width Clarke flow by a single-valued discrete backpropagation rule and updated the dependent observables.
- Promoted the zero-diversity kernel claim, the conditional mean-field claim, the monomial case, and the linear policy-gradient claim to proved statements.
- Reposed the surviving finite-width, rare-diversity, limit-interchange, exploration, and structural feature-map problems.
- Corrected the logistic-loss, smoothness, residual, NTK, extrapolation, and reinforcement-learning comparisons; the boundary residual is now the starter problem.
