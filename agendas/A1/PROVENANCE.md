# Provenance of MAIS-A1

This agenda is an experiment in AI-written mathematics. It was written by **Claude Fable 5** (Anthropic) under Lionel Levine's direction. A separate AI agent first reviewed the draft adversarially for precision, accessibility, and literature coverage. **GPT 5.6 Sol** (OpenAI) then audited the pre-repair draft: it checked the proved and background results, examined each numbered research item, and conducted literature searches testing its open-status claims.

The [pre-repair audit](../audit/2026-07/sol/MAIS-A1.md) and [scoped verification summary](../audit/2026-07/verification/MAIS-A1-summary.md) are public. The current edition incorporates the accepted findings recorded in the [repair log](../audit/2026-07/REPAIR-LOG.md#mais-a1-quantitative-bounded-löb) and passed the [final integration gate](../audit/2026-07/GATE-SUMMARY.md). The audit's central finding — a refutation and repair of the source parametric bounded Löb theorem — is published in full as [MAIS-P2](../../papers/P2/MAIS-P2.pdf), with its own [provenance](../../papers/P2/PROVENANCE.md).

AI agreement is not human peer review. No human has verified every line of this file. If you find an error, or believe a problem stated here as open has been resolved, please [open an issue](https://github.com/lionellevine/MAIS/issues/new/choose); corrections are credited.
